About

Analyst first,
retention-minded always.

I'm a data analyst at Megalotto, a leading gaming company across Nigeria. I work with Python, SQL, and Excel to analyze sales data trends, track terminal activities, and monitor player behavior across our network. My focus is turning operational data into actionable insights — understanding player retention, identifying risk patterns, and optimizing regional performance.

At Megalotto, I handle:

  • Terminal Analytics: Track terminal-level performance (revenue, transaction volume, uptime) across all states in Nigeria
  • Sales Trend Analysis: Identify seasonal patterns, regional winners/underperformers, and growth opportunities
  • Player Risk Segmentation: Classify players as high stackers, medium stackers, and casual players; flag at-risk cohorts for intervention
  • State-by-State Reporting: Monthly dashboards and reports by state showing revenue, player activity, terminal health
  • Retention & Churn Modeling: Predict which players are likely to go inactive; recommend retention campaigns

I care about the full path from query to recommendation — not just building a clean dataset, but explaining what it means for the business and what to do next. In gaming, this means understanding not just who plays, but how, when, and why they stop.
