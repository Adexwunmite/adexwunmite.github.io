Player Risk Segmentation & Terminal Performance Analysis

Company Megalotto (Gaming, Nigeria)
Tools SQL, Python, Power BI
Scope 36 states, 1000+ terminals, 50K+ players

At Megalotto, I segment players by risk profile (High Stackers, Medium Stackers, Casual Players) and track terminal-level performance across Nigeria. This analysis identifies at-risk cohorts for intervention, flags underperforming terminals, and informs state-by-state strategy.

The Business Challenge

Megalotto operates a network of 1,000+ gaming terminals across Nigeria, serving diverse player types. The challenge: not all players are the same risk, and not all terminals perform the same. We needed to:

  • Understand which players are high-value, at-risk, or casual, so retention can be targeted
  • Identify terminals or regions underperforming and needing intervention
  • Detect early signs of player churn before it happens
  • Segment by state to enable regional decision-making
  • Flag responsible gambling concerns in high-stacker cohorts

Without this segmentation, efforts were one-size-fits-all. With it, we could allocate resources smarter and predict problems earlier.

My Approach

1. Define Player Segments

Classified players into three buckets based on play frequency, average bet size, and total spend:

🔥 High Stackers
22.3%
Frequent players (3+ times/week), high avg bet. Highest spend, fastest churn when losing streaks hit.
📊 Medium Stackers
45.1%
Regular players (1-2 times/week), moderate spend. Steady retention, responsive to campaigns.
🎲 Casual Players
32.6%
Occasional (1-2 times/month), low spend. High natural churn, low reactivation ROI.

2. Risk Scoring

Built a churn risk score for each player using:

  • Days since last play: 30+ days = higher risk
  • Recent trend: Declining play frequency or bet size = risk signal
  • Losing streak: Extended losses correlate with churn, especially for High Stackers
  • Seasonality: Account for natural dips (holiday periods, etc.)

3. Terminal Performance Analysis

Aggregated player data by terminal to measure:

  • Monthly revenue and transaction volume
  • Player count (active, inactive, new)
  • Average session length and bet size
  • Health score (revenue vs. baseline for that region)

4. State-by-State Breakdown

Rolled up metrics by state for regional leadership: Lagos performs differently than Kano; insights by state drive different strategies.

Impact & Next Steps

Implemented Actions:

  • Launched targeted SMS campaigns to High Stackers showing early churn signals (7-day retention focus)
  • Built weekly alert system: flags terminals with 15%+ revenue drop for ops team investigation
  • Created state-level dashboards for regional managers to monitor local KPIs
  • Enhanced responsible gambling messaging for High Stackers (loss limits, cool-off reminders)
  • Automated monthly state-by-state report (saves 4 hours of manual work)

Results: 18% reactivation rate on Medium Stacker campaigns, early intervention on 4 underperforming states led to hardware/strategy fixes, churn tracking now proactive instead of reactive.

SQL & Python Behind the Scenes

Sample queries/code used to build this analysis:

SQL: Segment players by risk
WITH player_metrics AS ( SELECT player_id, COUNT(DISTINCT DATE(play_date)) AS days_active_30d, SUM(bet_amount) AS total_spend_30d, AVG(bet_amount) AS avg_bet, MAX(play_date) AS last_play, DATEDIFF(day, MAX(play_date), CAST(GETDATE() AS DATE)) AS days_since_play FROM player_transactions WHERE play_date >= DATEADD(day, -30, CAST(GETDATE() AS DATE)) GROUP BY player_id ) SELECT player_id, CASE WHEN days_active_30d >= 20 AND avg_bet > 500 THEN 'High Stacker' WHEN days_active_30d >= 8 AND avg_bet > 200 THEN 'Medium Stacker' ELSE 'Casual Player' END AS segment, total_spend_30d, days_since_play, CASE WHEN days_since_play > 30 THEN 'HIGH' WHEN days_since_play > 14 THEN 'MEDIUM' ELSE 'LOW' END AS churn_risk FROM player_metrics;
Python: Automated weekly risk report
import pandas as pd import pyodbc # Connect to SQL, fetch risk data conn = pyodbc.connect('Driver={SQL Server};Server=...;Database=megalotto') query = "SELECT * FROM player_risk_weekly WHERE churn_risk='HIGH'" at_risk = pd.read_sql(query, conn) # Group by state, summarize state_summary = at_risk.groupby('state').agg({ 'player_id': 'count', 'total_spend_30d': 'sum' }).rename(columns={'player_id': 'at_risk_count'}) # Generate report email report = f"High-risk players by state:\n{state_summary.to_string()}" # Send email to stakeholders send_email(to='retention_team@megalotto.com', subject='Weekly Risk Alert', body=report)
